FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in Barcelona?

Turnkey from €7,000 without appliances. The price depends on size, cabinet door and worktop materials, and moving water and electrics.

How long does it take?

Usually 3–5 weeks. The longest wait is the made-to-measure kitchen, so we order it right after measuring.

Can the kitchen be opened to the living room?

Yes, if the wall is not load-bearing. In Barcelona this needs a comunicat immediat — work can start right after filing. We help with it.

Can I keep my appliances?

Yes. We build the units around their sizes.

What languages do you speak?

English, Spanish, Russian and Ukrainian.
