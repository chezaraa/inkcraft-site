FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does an apartment renovation cost in Barcelona?

A refresh starts at €350/m², a turnkey renovation at €650/m², with a design project at €1,000/m². The exact figure comes after a free visit and is fixed in the contract. What drives the price →

What is the difference between a cosmetic and a full renovation?

Cosmetic means finishes: paint, floors, doors, lighting. A full renovation replaces electrics and pipes and rebuilds the kitchen and bathroom. Cosmetic renovation →

How long does it take?

Example: a 30 m² studio, turnkey, took 3 months from demolition to keys. Typical timelines →

Do I need a permit?

Finishes only: a simple notification (assabentat). Moving non-load-bearing partitions: comunicat immediat. Structural work or a protected building: comunicat diferit or a major works licence. Details →

I live abroad. Does that work?

Yes. We agree on everything via WhatsApp and send photos and videos every week. How it works →

What languages do you speak?

English, Spanish, Russian and Ukrainian.
