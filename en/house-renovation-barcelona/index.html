FAQ

Answers up front

Do you work outside Barcelona?

Yes — in the suburbs and along the coast: Castelldefels, Gavà, Sitges and beyond. Send us the address.

Do I need a permit?

Interior finishes: usually a simple notification. Façade, roof, layout changes or a pool need more. We tell you in advance. Permits →

How much goes to the council?

ICIO tax up to 4% of the works cost, plus a fee and a refundable waste deposit. More →

It’s a holiday let. Can you work between bookings?

Yes. We plan the work around your bookings and send photos every week.

Can you assess a house before I buy?

Yes, we can come to a viewing and give a rough renovation figure. What to check →
