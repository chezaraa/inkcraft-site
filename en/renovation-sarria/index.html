About the area

What matters when renovating in Sarrià

Sarrià-Sant Gervasi is one of Barcelona’s most sought-after areas: spacious flats, houses with gardens, international schools nearby.

  • Large flats

    Flats of 100 m² and more with several bathrooms. One team, one schedule.

  • Houses in old Sarrià

    A former village: old houses with thick walls, patios and gardens. We work carefully with the structure.

  • High standards

    Clients here expect high quality: natural materials, made-to-measure furniture, well-planned lighting.

  • Design project

    For large flats a design project pays for itself. If we do the renovation, the design fee is deducted. Interior design →

  • The family lives nearby

    Families often rent nearby during the works. We keep to the schedule so you don’t pay extra months of rent.

  • Quiet streets

    A quiet area: we respect quiet hours and keep the stairwell clean.
