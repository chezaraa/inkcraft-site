About the city

What matters when renovating in L’Hospitalet

Many buildings from the second half of the 20th century have never been renovated. Neighbourhoods include Collblanc, La Torrassa, Santa Eulàlia and Bellvitge.

  • Renovation from scratch

    Flats are often in original condition: old wiring, old pipes, 1980s tiles. We do everything turnkey.

  • Its own council

    L’Hospitalet is a separate city; permits go to its own Ajuntament (city council). We’ll tell you what’s needed and prepare the documents. Council fees →

  • Rental demand

    Being close to Barcelona and the Fira exhibition centre keeps rental demand high. We renovate with the return in mind.

  • Budget

    We can do it well without overspending: mid-range materials where they don’t show, accents where they do.

  • Windows

    New windows mean quiet and lower air-conditioning bills. Windows →

  • Before you buy

    We can view the flat before you sign and give you a renovation guide price.
