From our work

It started with four roof tiles

The owner of a holiday-let house called us to replace four roof tiles: the roof was leaking. We found the cause — pine needles had blocked the gutter and water was running under the tiles. We cleaned it, replaced the tiles, and the leak stopped.

A week later his wife called. We then repainted the whole house, redid two bathrooms and the kitchen, closed one of two kitchen doorways, widened the other and assembled all the new furniture.

That’s why no job is too small for us. Do a small job well, and you’ll know who to trust with a big one.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will you take a really small job?

Yes. Send photos and a description on WhatsApp. If it’s very small, we can combine it with other jobs in one visit.

How much does it cost?

Painting from €12/m², tiling from €45/m². Labour only, materials extra. We give a rough price from photos and an exact one after a visit.

My materials or yours?

Either. We can buy them or work with yours.

Can you widen or close a doorway?

Yes, if the wall isn’t load-bearing. That counts as a layout change, so Barcelona requires a comunicat. We help with it.

Do you work on holiday-let properties?

Yes. We can work between guest stays and send photos if you’re not around.
