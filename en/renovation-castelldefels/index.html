About the town

What matters when renovating in Castelldefels

Many expats and families have moved here from Barcelona to be closer to the sea. From the beach to the hillside areas of Bellamar and Montmar.

  • Flats by the beach

    Humidity and sand: hard-wearing floors, stainless hardware, good bathroom ventilation.

  • Houses and townhouses

    More space, more work: several bathrooms, stairs, terraces. One team does it all. House renovation →

  • Terraces

    The terrace is a second living room: surface, lighting, pergolas, an outdoor kitchen. Terraces →

  • Families with children

    We plan storage, safe materials and a kitchen that works for the whole family.

  • Not moved in yet?

    We can run the renovation remotely with weekly updates.

  • Permits

    Works in Castelldefels are approved by the local council (Ajuntament de Castelldefels). We’ll tell you what’s needed.
