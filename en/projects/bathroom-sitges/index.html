What we did

Stone, marble and brass

A calm bathroom in natural tones with one bold accent — a marble-look shower wall.

  • Large-format tiles

    Walls and floor in large stone-look slabs: fewer joints, the bathroom looks bigger.

  • Marble-look shower wall

    A veined accent wall, brass shower column and screen frame.

  • Vanity and basin

    Green floating vanity with a wood-look top, fluted glass basin, tall brass tap.

  • Towel rail

    Designer electric towel rail.

  • Lighting and sockets

    Spotlights and a socket block by the mirror.

  • 3D design

    The client approved the look before materials were bought — no surprises at the end.
