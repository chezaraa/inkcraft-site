How it went

The owner lived in Dublin. He arrived at a finished apartment.

  • The window order went astray for a month. The supplier’s manager left and nobody took over the order. We tracked it down and got production going.
  • The Italian doors were held up. The supplier suspected fraud and asked for paperwork. We sent it, and the doors arrived.
  • We ordered and took delivery of all 47 items. The owner didn’t have to handle a single box.
  • 3 months from demolition to handover.

Had he run the renovation himself, he would only have found out about all this on arrival.

What we did

A full apartment in 30 m²

Open zones instead of solid walls let light and air flow through.

  • Sleeping alcove

    A shelving partition screens off the bed, creating a bedroom without blocking the light.

  • Kitchen in an alcove

    Made-to-measure units, built-in appliances, marble-look splashback, with a round backlit mirror beside it.

  • Bathroom

    Glass shower, wall-hung toilet, wooden wall panel, vanity with open shelves, lit mirror.

  • Windows and doors

    We ordered, chased and fitted the new windows and Italian doors.

  • Lighting

    Spotlights and accent lighting make a small flat feel bigger.

  • Materials

    47 items: chosen, ordered, received, checked. The client approved every choice from photos on WhatsApp.
