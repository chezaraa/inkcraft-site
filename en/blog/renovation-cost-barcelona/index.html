How much does an apartment renovation cost in Barcelona in 2026

Prices per square metre, the bathroom and kitchen separately, and what moves the final quote the most.

In short: a turnkey apartment renovation in Barcelona in 2026 costs roughly €300–1,400 per m² depending on the level (mid-range turnkey €500–700/m²), more with premium materials and made-to-measure furniture. A 70 m² apartment: roughly €28,000–70,000. Below: what the price is made of and where you can save.

Bathroom and kitchen separately

  • Turnkey bathroom (4–6 m²): usually €3,500–8,000 on the market. Ours from €5,000; shower instead of a bath from €6,500. More →
  • Turnkey kitchen (no appliances): usually €6,000–15,000. Ours from €7,000; open-plan from €12,000. More →

The kitchen and bathroom are the most expensive square metres: water, electrics, tiles and furniture all meet there. That’s why a 30 m² studio almost always costs more per m² than a 90 m² apartment.

What affects the price most

  1. Pipes and wiring. In old buildings in Eixample, Gràcia or the Gothic Quarter everything often has to be replaced back to the risers. You only see this on a visit.
  2. Layout changes. Moving walls, the kitchen or the bathroom adds work and needs a permit. Which permit →
  3. Materials. The same bathroom can cost 5 000 or €12,000 — the difference is tiles, sanitaryware and taps.
  4. Made-to-measure furniture. Kitchen, floor-to-ceiling wardrobes, partitions — a big part of the budget, but they make an apartment look expensive.
  5. Logistics. No lift, narrow stairs, a skip on the street — all show up in the quote.
  6. Taxes and permits. If the work needs a council permit, ICIO tax (up to 4%) and a fee are added. Council fees →

How not to overpay

  • Design first, build second. Decisions made on the fly are the main cause of budget overruns. Our design fee is credited against the renovation.
  • A fixed quote in the contract. The price should be fixed before work starts, not “as we go”.
  • Don’t move walls unless you need to. A good layout within existing walls saves money and permit time.
  • Spend where hands touch. Taps, handles, worktops. For hidden items, mid-range is fine.

How long it takes

Bathroom 2–4 weeks, kitchen 3–5 weeks, 30 m² studio turnkey about 3 months. See our projects with timelines →
