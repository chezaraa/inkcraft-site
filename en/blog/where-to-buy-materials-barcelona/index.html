Where to buy renovation materials in Barcelona

Superstores, showrooms, trade suppliers — what to buy where, and five mistakes that push the budget up.

In short: basic materials are sold by large DIY superstores in Barcelona, tiles and sanitaryware are best chosen in showrooms, and large volumes are cheaper through trade suppliers. But the most common overspend isn’t price — it’s wrong quantities and delivery times.

Where to buy

  • DIY superstores (Leroy Merlin, Bricomart, BAUHAUS) — adhesives, paint, tools, basic tiles and sanitaryware. Handy for small items and urgent top-ups. Bricomart has more trade-size packs.
  • Tile and bathroom showrooms — more choice, real samples, collections that aren’t in stock. Spanish tiles are among the best in Europe; most factories are in Castellón province.
  • Trade suppliers (almacenes de materiales) — cement, plasterboard, profiles, pipes. Mostly for builders; they deliver to site.
  • IKEA and joiners — kitchens and wardrobes. Made-to-measure costs more but fits exactly. Kitchen renovation →

Five mistakes with materials

  1. No spare tiles. Order +10% (+15% for diagonal laying). A month later the same batch may be gone — and the shade will differ.
  2. Mixed batches. Check the batch number (lote) and calibre on the boxes.
  3. Delivery times. Special-order tiles, shower trays, doors, kitchens — 2 to 8 weeks. Order right after the design, not when the crew is waiting.
  4. Getting it upstairs. Old Barcelona buildings have narrow stairs and small lifts. Large-format tiles or shower glass may not fit.
  5. Indoor materials outside. On a terrace, only frost-resistant, non-slip tiles. Terraces →
