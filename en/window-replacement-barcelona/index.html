FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit to replace windows?

Windows are part of the façade. Same look and colour: usually a notification to the council. Different colour, glazing bars or opening size: you need the owners’ association’s consent and sometimes a licence. We check before ordering. Renovation and neighbours →

PVC or aluminium?

PVC is warmer and usually cheaper. Aluminium is slimmer, stronger and better for large sliding terrace doors.

Will it be quieter?

Yes, noticeably. Double glazing cuts street noise well — in central Barcelona that’s often the main reason to replace windows.

How long does it take?

Manufacturing takes a few weeks after measuring. Installation is usually one or two days per apartment.

What languages do you speak?

English, Spanish, Russian and Ukrainian.
