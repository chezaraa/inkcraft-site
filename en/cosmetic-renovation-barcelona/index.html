FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between cosmetic and full renovation?

Cosmetic is finishes: walls, floors, doors, lighting, fittings in the same places. A full renovation replaces electrics and pipes, rebuilds the kitchen and bathroom and sometimes changes the layout.

Do I need a permit?

Without layout changes, Barcelona usually requires a simple notification (assabentat). Permits →

Do I pay ICIO tax?

In Barcelona, interior work that doesn’t change layout, structure or façade is usually not subject to ICIO (the local construction tax). Council fees →

How long does it take?

Usually 1–2 months for a 50–80 m² apartment.

Can I live there during the work?

Possible if we go room by room, but faster and cleaner when the apartment is empty.
