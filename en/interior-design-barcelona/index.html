Design supervision

Why we don’t charge for supervision on our own projects

When we both design and build, the designer and the crew are one team — checking the work against the drawings is part of the renovation. If another contractor builds to our design, we can supervise them: €1,500 per month.

If a licence with an architect’s project is needed (removing load-bearing walls, changing the façade), the law requires the project and the site management (dirección de obra) to be signed by an architect. We help you find one and build to their project.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does an interior design project cost?

Layout from €10/m², technical project from €25/m², full design with 3D and material selection from €40/m².

Is the design fee credited against the renovation?

Yes. If we do the renovation, we deduct the design fee from the renovation price.

Do I need design supervision?

Not if we build it: design and construction are one team. If another contractor builds, we can supervise them for €1,500 per month.

Can we work remotely?

Yes. We present the layout, 3D and drawings online and take revisions on WhatsApp.
