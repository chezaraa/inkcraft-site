    FAQ

    Answers up front

    How much does a renovation cost?

    It depends on the size, the condition of the apartment and the materials. We give an exact figure after the free visit: quote within 24 hours, then the price is fixed in the contract.

    How long does it take?

    Example: a 30 m² studio, turnkey, took 3 months from demolition to keys. The timeline for your apartment is written in the quote.

    I live abroad. Does this work?

    Yes. Decisions are agreed on WhatsApp, you get updates every week, and we receive the materials ourselves. No need to come for every stage.

    Can I have just the building work, without a design project?

    Yes. We work from your examples, from your designer's project, or we create the project ourselves, whichever suits you.

    Do I need a permit?

    Many works in Spain need a permit or prior notice to the Ayuntamiento (town hall). We'll tell you which one applies and help you get it.

    What warranty do you give?

    3 years on the work we carry out.

    Which languages do you speak?

    English, Spanish, Russian and Ukrainian.
